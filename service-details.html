Comprehensive Overview

Live Deg Theater for Your Grand Day

A wedding feast in the Deccan is incomplete without the intoxicating scent of saffron basmati bursting from a newly unsealed copper deg.

We bring the complete royal kitchen to your wedding venue. Our master ustads set up live coal stations where handis are opened in front of your guests, releasing plumes of cardamom, rose water, and braised meat steam.

From initial menu tastings at our restaurant to customized brass ware and synchronized banquet service, we manage every detail with the utmost royal grandeur.
