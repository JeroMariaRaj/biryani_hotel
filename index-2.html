Our 4-Stage Sacred Ritual

The Four Pillars of Authentic Dum Cooking

Stage 01
Marination & Yakhni

Meats are soaked in hung curd, ginger garlic paste, and crushed green chillies for 12 hours to break down collagen naturally.

Stage 02
Precision Rice Par-Boil

2-year aged basmati is boiled in rolling aromatic water with cinnamon bark, cloves and green cardamom to precisely 70% tenderness.

Stage 03
Dough Seal (Pukht)

The heavy brass deg lid is hermetically sealed with fresh whole-wheat dough to trap every trace of steam and vapor inside the pot.

Stage 04
Gentle Charcoal Embers

Slow-simmered over wood embers from below and glowing coals from above, creating a radiant convection heat chamber.
