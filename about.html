Since 1984

From Nizam Court Secrets to Global Recognition

Founded by Ustad Hafizuddin Qureshi in the historic Charminar bazaar, Zaffran was born from a singular passion: restoring the lost grandeur of authentic royal dum cooking.

In the 1980s, commercial pressure led many restaurants to adopt speed cooking with pressure cookers and chemical colorings. Ustad Hafizuddin refused. He insisted that true biryani can only be coaxed to life in thick copper handis, sealed with fresh dough and cradled over smoldering wood embers.

Today, four decades later, his sons and grandchildren oversee four royal dining hubs and an extensive express catering network, preparing over 2,500 authentic handis each day without losing a single degree of traditional craftsmanship.

The Sacred Technique

The Philosophy of 'Dum Pukht'

Dum Pukht literally translates to 'breathing in one's own steam'. It is an ancient Persian-Awadhi culinary secret that refuses shortcuts.

Copper provides uniform heat distribution without hot spots, preventing the rice from scorching while allowing the bottom layers to develop that cherished golden crispness known as tahchin.

By sealing the rim with fresh whole-wheat atta dough, volatile aromatic essential oils from cardamom, mace, and rose petals are re-condensed back into the rice grains rather than vanishing into the kitchen air.

Coals placed directly on top of the copper lid create radiant downward heat, ensuring the delicate topmost basmati layer is gently steamed to a dry, fluffy, separation-of-grain perfection.
