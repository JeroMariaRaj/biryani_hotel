A Living Heritage of Slow-Cooked Perfection

At Zaffran, biryani is not just a dish — it is an age-old ritual celebrated across generations.

Our master ustads trace their lineage to the royal kitchens of the Deccan. We preserve the authentic Dum Pukht technique: heavy-bottomed copper degs are hand-sealed with dough, ensuring no steam escapes while the meats tenderize in their own natural juices.
